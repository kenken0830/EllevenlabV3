# QA rubric

## Score 1 to 5
1 = weak
3 = acceptable
5 = strong

## Categories
### hook_strength
Does the opening make a listener want to continue?

### low_explainer_tone
Does it avoid lecture-like explanation?

### spoken_naturalness
Would this sound natural if spoken aloud in Japanese?

### emotion_curve
Does the emotional temperature move naturally?

### eleven_prompt_naturalness
Is the ElevenLabs v3 prompt practical, restrained, and natural?

## Common failure reasons
- opening starts with explanation instead of empathy
- too many pauses or ellipses
- every sentence lands too hard
- tag-heavy delivery
- polished writing instead of spoken Japanese
- strong idea in brief but flat spoken_script
- brief body plan already reads like an advice column or mini lesson
- brief close contains more than one action or carries too much teaching weight
- spoken-script body reads like seminar notes or an article summary
- spoken-script body uses explicit teaching markers such as "まず", "次に", "つまり", or "大切なのは"
- spoken-script body feels overstructured instead of continuing as conversation
